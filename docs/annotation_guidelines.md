# ScriptoNet Annotation Guidelines v1.0

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this annotation framework is to transform literary passages into structured data for computational analysis.

The framework identifies representations of therapeutic processes in literary narratives.

The annotation does not identify real psychological conditions. It examines how fiction represents emotional processing, reflection, and transformation.

---

# 2. Annotation Unit

The basic annotation unit is a literary passage.

Each passage is evaluated independently while considering sufficient narrative context.

Each passage receives:

- Therapeutic representation label
- Therapeutic mechanism
- Intensity score
- Annotator reasoning

---

# 3. Primary Classification

## Therapeutic Representation Label

Question:

Does this passage represent emotional processing, healing, reflection, or transformation?

## Label 1: Present

Assign label 1 when the passage contains:

- emotional processing
- meaning-making
- self-reflection leading to understanding
- narrative reconstruction
- emotional release through expression
- supportive interactions that contribute to emotional regulation


## Label 0: Not Present

Assign label 0 when the passage contains:

- traumatic events only
- sadness without processing
- physical descriptions
- ordinary conversations
- emotional reactions without transformation

---

# 4. Therapeutic Mechanisms

## Narrative Reconstruction

Definition:

A character reorganizes past experiences into a meaningful personal narrative.

Indicators:

- reflection on past events
- connecting past and present
- understanding identity changes


## Emotional Catharsis

Definition:

Emotional expression or interpersonal exchange that allows release or regulation of distress.

Indicators:

- expressing hidden emotions
- receiving emotional support
- release of internal suffering


## Scriptotherapy

Definition:

Writing or storytelling used as a means of emotional processing.

Indicators:

- letters
- journals
- confession
- personal narratives


## Poetry-mediated Processing

Definition:

Poetic expression used for emotional understanding or transformation.


## Music-mediated Processing

Definition:

Music or artistic sound experiences used for emotional regulation or reflection.

---

# 5. Intensity Scale

1 - Very weak

Minimal indication of therapeutic meaning.

2 - Weak

Some emotional reflection but limited processing.

3 - Moderate

Clear emotional processing or meaning-making.

4 - Strong

Deep reflection and significant narrative transformation.

5 - Very strong

Major emotional transformation or resolution.

---

# 6. Annotation Principles

Important rules:

Emotion alone does not equal therapeutic representation.

Trauma alone does not equal healing.

Memory alone does not equal narrative reconstruction.

The annotation focuses on the function of the narrative, not only the presence of emotional words.

---

# 7. Quality Control

Annotations should include reasoning notes explaining why a label was assigned.

Examples should be reviewed to ensure consistency between literary interpretation and computational categories.
