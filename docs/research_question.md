# Research Question

## Project Title

ScriptoNet: AI-Assisted Detection and Mapping of Therapeutic Processes in Literary Fiction


## Main Research Question

Can Artificial Intelligence assist literary scholars in identifying and mapping representations of therapeutic processes in literary narratives while preserving humanistic interpretation?


## Research Motivation

Literary fiction often represents complex human experiences such as:

- trauma
- memory
- guilt
- emotional conflict
- suffering
- healing
- identity transformation

Literary scholars traditionally study these themes through close reading and interpretation.

However, close reading is time-intensive and difficult to apply across large collections of texts.

ScriptoNet explores how Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing can support literary analysis by identifying patterns in large-scale literary collections.


## Research Perspective

This project does not aim to replace literary scholars.

Instead, Artificial Intelligence is used as a supporting research tool that can:

- identify possible therapeutic representations
- organise large textual collections
- discover linguistic patterns
- support human interpretation


## Research Boundary

This project studies the representation of therapeutic processes inside literary texts.

It does not:

- diagnose mental health conditions
- measure clinical outcomes
- claim that literature provides medical therapy

The focus is computational analysis of literary representation.
